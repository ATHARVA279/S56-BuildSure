BUILDSURE SAMPLE BUILDING CODE - PART 12: SITE WELFARE AND PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT
Document type: building code
NOTE: Fictional sample content for demonstration only. Not a real regulation.

Section 12.1 Scope
This part applies to all construction sites and sets minimum welfare facilities and personal protective equipment (PPE) requirements.

Section 12.2 Head Protection
Safety helmets shall be worn by all persons on site, except in site offices and welfare areas. Helmets shall be replaced after any significant impact or after 5 years from the date of manufacture.

Section 12.3 Foot Protection
Safety footwear with steel or composite toe caps and puncture-resistant soles shall be worn at all times in work areas.

Section 12.4 Eye and Hearing Protection
Eye protection is required when cutting, grinding or chipping. Hearing protection is required wherever noise exceeds 85 dB(A) over an 8-hour period.

Section 12.5 First Aid
A first aid box shall be provided for every 25 workers, and a trained first aider shall be present on site whenever more than 5 persons are working. First aid boxes shall be checked monthly.

Section 12.6 Toilets and Washing
There shall be at least one toilet for every 15 workers and washing facilities with hot and cold water close to the toilets. Facilities shall be cleaned daily.

Section 12.7 Drinking Water
A supply of wholesome drinking water shall be provided at accessible points. Where temperatures exceed 35 degrees Celsius, shaded rest areas shall also be provided.
