BUILDSURE SAMPLE BUILDING CODE - PART 9: ELECTRICAL INSTALLATIONS ON SITE
Document type: building code
NOTE: Fictional sample content for demonstration only. Not a real regulation.

Section 9.1 Scope
This part covers temporary and permanent electrical installations in buildings under construction.

Section 9.2 Earthing
All metallic enclosures, scaffolding and site cabins shall be bonded to a common earth. Earth electrode resistance shall not exceed 5 ohms, measured before energising.

Section 9.3 Residual Current Protection
Every temporary socket outlet on site shall be protected by a residual current device (RCD) with a rated tripping current not exceeding 30 mA. RCDs shall be tested weekly by pressing the test button and the result logged.

Section 9.4 Cable Protection
Cables laid on the ground shall be protected against vehicle damage by covers or ducts. Buried cables shall be at least 600 mm deep and marked with warning tape 150 mm above the cable.

Section 9.5 Cable Heights
Overhead cables crossing site access routes shall be at least 5.5 m above ground. Elsewhere on site, overhead cables shall be at least 3.5 m above ground.

Section 9.6 Distribution Boards
Distribution boards shall be weatherproof with an ingress rating of at least IP54 and shall be locked. Each outgoing circuit shall be labelled.

Section 9.7 Inspection Interval
Temporary installations shall be inspected and tested by a competent person at least every 3 months, and after any modification.
