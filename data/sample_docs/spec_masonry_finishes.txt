PROJECT SPECIFICATION - MASONRY AND FINISHES, TOWER B
Document type: project specification
Project: Harbour View Tower B (fictional)
NOTE: Fictional sample content for demonstration only.

Clause MF-1 Block Strength
Concrete blocks for external walls shall have a minimum compressive strength of 7.5 MPa. Blocks for internal partitions shall have a minimum strength of 3.5 MPa.

Clause MF-2 Mortar Mix
This clause overrides Section 5.3 of the sample building code for external walls of Tower B. Mortar for all external masonry shall have a cement to sand ratio of 1:4 and shall include a waterproofing admixture at the manufacturer's recommended dose.

Clause MF-3 Partition Thickness
Internal partition walls shall be 150 mm thick in common corridors and 100 mm thick inside apartments.

Clause MF-4 Plaster Thickness
Internal plaster shall be applied in two coats to a total thickness of 15 mm. External plaster shall be applied in two coats to a total thickness of 20 mm.

Clause MF-5 Plaster Curing
External plaster shall be kept damp for at least 7 days. In ambient temperatures above 35 degrees Celsius, curing shall be extended to 10 days.

Clause MF-6 Floor Tile Fixing
Floor tiles shall be fixed with polymer-modified adhesive over a screed cured for at least 14 days. Tile joints shall be 3 mm wide and grouted after 24 hours.

Clause MF-7 Paint System
External walls shall receive one coat of primer and two coats of weather-resistant acrylic emulsion. Application shall not take place when relative humidity exceeds 85 percent.
