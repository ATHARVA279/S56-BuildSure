Finding B-1 Rebar Spacing
Main bars in the Level 3 transfer slab were measured at 175 mm centres in the east bay. Clause CW-3 of the concrete works specification requires a maximum of 150 mm for this slab, so this does not comply, even though it would satisfy the general 200 mm limit in Section 4.2 of the building code. Status: open. Action: contractor to add bars to achieve 150 mm spacing and request re-inspection.

Finding B-2 Concrete Cover
External face cover was measured at 46 mm, which meets Clause CW-4 requiring 45 mm. Status: closed.

Finding B-3 Curing
Curing records showed only 5 days of wet curing on the Level 3 slab at an ambient temperature of 36 degrees Celsius. Clause CW-5 requires 10 days above 35 degrees. Status: open. Action: extend curing and submit revised records.

Finding B-4 Excavation Edge Protection
A barrier around the basement excavation was found only 0.6 m from the edge. Clause EF-3 requires at least 1.0 m. Status: open. Action: relocate barrier immediately.

Finding B-5 Cube Results
Seven-day cube results for the Level 3 pour averaged 24 MPa, which is consistent with a grade M30 mix reaching its target strength at 28 days. Status: closed.

Finding B-6 Formwork Removal
Soffit formwork on Level 3 was struck at 8 days. Clause CW-7 requires a minimum of 10 days and 70 percent strength. Status: open. Action: formwork to be re-propped and engineer to review.
