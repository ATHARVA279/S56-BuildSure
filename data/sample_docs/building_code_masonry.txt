BUILDSURE SAMPLE BUILDING CODE - PART 5: MASONRY
Document type: building code
NOTE: Fictional sample content for demonstration only. Not a real regulation.

Section 5.1 Scope
This part applies to load-bearing and non-load-bearing masonry walls built from clay brick, concrete block or stone in buildings up to 4 storeys.

Section 5.2 Minimum Wall Thickness
Load-bearing external walls shall be at least 230 mm thick. Non-load-bearing internal partition walls shall be at least 100 mm thick.

Section 5.3 Mortar Mix
Mortar for load-bearing masonry shall have a cement to sand ratio of 1:4 by volume. Mortar for non-load-bearing partitions may use a ratio of 1:6. Mortar shall be used within 2 hours of mixing.

Section 5.4 Joint Thickness
Mortar joints shall be between 8 mm and 12 mm thick. Horizontal joints shall be fully filled and vertical joints shall be filled for the full height of the unit.

Section 5.5 Wall Height Limit
The height of an unsupported masonry wall shall not exceed 20 times its thickness. Walls exceeding this ratio shall be braced by piers, cross walls or a ring beam.

Section 5.6 Damp-Proof Course
A damp-proof course shall be provided at least 150 mm above finished ground level and shall extend through the full thickness of the wall.

Section 5.7 Curing of Masonry
Newly built masonry shall be kept damp for at least 7 days and protected from rain and direct sun during that period.
